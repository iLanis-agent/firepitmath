Fire pit math

A backyard fire pit goes wrong in three places: the block count that runs short on the last course, the gravel base that was never sized before the bags were bought, and the ring that turns out to sit too close to the fence once the chairs arrive. Fire pit math turns the diameter into the blocks, turns the margin into the gravel, and turns the space into an honest verdict. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Ring plan - the diameter around the circle becomes the blocks per course and the stack.
  • Gravel plan - the diameter plus a labeled margin becomes the base area, the liters and the bags.
  • Clearance plan - the diameter plus a labeled clearance becomes the clear circle, checked against your space.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 40 cm block face, 3 courses, a 30 cm gravel margin each side, a 10 cm base depth, 25 L bags and a 3 m clearance each side - all illustrative defaults you can change by measuring. Real fire pits follow the local fire rules, the burn ban of the day and the clearances your code asks; a fire is never left alone. This is planning arithmetic, not a fire permit.
